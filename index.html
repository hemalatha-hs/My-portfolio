Incremental ingestion

Auto Loader and Structured Streaming load CSV and JSON into Bronze Delta tables, capturing source-file metadata and ingestion timestamps.

Quality controls

PySpark transformations standardize data and check nulls, duplicates, datatypes, and foreign keys. Rejected records are stored separately.

Historical modeling

Fact and dimension tables follow a star schema. SCD Type 2 retains historical changes, with aggregated data marts for analysis.

Orchestration & reporting

Databricks Workflows manages task dependencies. Developer tests verify incremental updates, while SQL dashboards present KPIs and data quality metrics.
